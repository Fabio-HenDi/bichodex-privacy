BichoDex — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026
Contact: tengumori.apps@gmail.com

BichoDex is a game for identifying and collecting wildlife you photograph. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your data. The short version: your collection lives on your device, photos sent for identification are deleted immediately after processing, locations are never recorded precisely, and there are no user accounts.

Data stored on your device only

Everything that makes up your personal collection is stored locally on your device and is never sent to a BichoDex server:

  • The photos you capture.
  • Your list of identified animals and dog breeds, with dates.
  • The coarse location of each sighting (see "Location" below).
  • Any notes you add to a sighting.
  • Achievement progress.

If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device. Depending on your Android settings it may still exist in a backup, either one you made yourself (see "Google Drive backup") or one Android made automatically (see "Android system backup").

Data sent off your device

Photos, for identification only

When you capture a photo and it is identified, a copy of that image (cropped to the framing guide) is sent over an encrypted connection to our identification service. There it is analysed to identify the animal and to check that it is a photo of a live animal rather than a photo of a screen or a printed picture. The image is processed and then deleted immediately. It is never stored on our servers. Your own full-resolution copy stays on your device.

Our identification service also uses Google's Gemini API as part of this process (for the live-photo check, for generating the species/breed facts shown in the app, and for creating the illustrated sketch for a species the first time anyone discovers it). The image and/or the species name are sent to Google for that purpose. Google processes this data as our service provider.

Approximate location

If you grant location permission, BichoDex records only a coarse region for each sighting — your location is snapped to a grid cell roughly 20 km across (or a named area) on your device, before it is stored or sent anywhere. A precise coordinate is never saved and never leaves your device. This is a deliberate safety choice: precise locations of wild animals can be misused.

The coarse region is:

  • Stored with the sighting on your device.
  • Sent to our service as part of an anonymous, aggregated count ("how many people have seen this species in this region"). These counts contain no identifier for you and no individual sighting record — the individual submission is discarded immediately after the count is incremented.

Location permission is optional. The app works fully without it; you just won't get the map, regional suggestions, or location on your sightings.

An anonymous installation identifier

When first launched, the app generates a random identifier for your installation. It is sent with requests to our identification service and is used only to prevent abuse of the service (for example, rate-limiting). It is not linked to your identity, your device's advertising ID, or any account, and it is not used for analytics or advertising. It is generated by the app itself and is not derived from any hardware or device identifier. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, Android may restore the previous identifier as part of its own system backup (see below); otherwise a new one is generated.

Reference photos

To show you what an identified species or breed looks like, the app loads a reference photo from public wildlife sources (iNaturalist for species, the Dog CEO API for dog breeds). Your device loads these images directly, so those services receive the ordinary connection information any website receives, including your IP address. No photo of yours, no location, no identifier from this app, and no account information is sent with these requests.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to back up your collection, BichoDex connects to your own Google Drive and stores the backup in a hidden, app-specific folder (appDataFolder) that only this app can see. The backup contains your collection data and photos. BichoDex operates no server that stores your backup — it goes directly from your device to your Google account. You can disconnect at any time from the app's Backup screen. Deleting the app's data from your Google account is done through Google Drive.

Android system backup

Separately from the above, Android's own backup feature is enabled for this app, which is the default for Android apps. If you have device backup turned on in your Android settings, the operating system may copy the app's local data to your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall. This is handled entirely by Android, goes to your account rather than to us, and can be turned off in your device's backup settings. BichoDex has no access to it.

What BichoDex does NOT do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or profiles.
  • No social features — no other user can see your collection, photos, location, or activity inside the app.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No precise/GPS location storage.
  • No server-side storage of your photos or your collection.

Children

BichoDex is suitable for a general audience including families and children. The app does not require any personal information to use, has no accounts, no social interaction between users, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. The only optional data collection (approximate region, anonymous install ID, photos processed-then-deleted for identification) is described above and applies the same way to all users.

Data retention

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Photos sent for identification: deleted immediately after processing.
  • Individual location submissions: discarded immediately after being added to an anonymous regional count.
  • Anonymous regional counts and the shared species dictionary: retained as aggregate, non-personal data.
  • Server logs may retain the anonymous install identifier and request metadata for a short period for abuse prevention and debugging.

Your choices

  • Location: grant or deny in your device settings at any time.
  • Backup: connect or disconnect Google Drive from the Backup screen.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the app removes all on-device data. Because there are no accounts, there is no server-side profile to delete; to request deletion of anonymous server logs, contact us at the address above.
